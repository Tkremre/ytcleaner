### 1.4.0

- Added **Only subscribed uploaders** to Subscriptions: keep collaborations uploaded by channels you follow and hide those published by channels you do not follow.
- Kept uploader checks lightweight with minimal metadata requests and in-memory caching. Videos remain visible if verification fails.
- Redesigned settings with **Videos**, **Subscriptions**, and **Layout** tabs, a distinct master switch, and a close button.
- Added discreet flask icons to experimental options, with explanations on hover.
- Improved keyboard navigation, focus handling, small-screen layout, and light/dark themes.
- Added filter status and regression coverage for paginated subscriptions, metadata failures, and channels with similar names.

The collaboration filter is **disabled by default**. Enable **Only subscribed uploaders** in the **Subscriptions** tab. Existing settings are preserved.
